## The invariant

Every HTTP route that returns or mutates data belonging to a user or a brand enforces that the caller owns that data.

Rationale: One tenant reading or writing another tenant's data is the worst failure this product can have.

## Method

1. Enumerate every route registration in server/. Search for app.get, app.post, app.put, app.patch, app.delete and router.* equivalents. There are about 261.
2. For each, determine whether the handler returns or mutates user-scoped or brand-scoped data.
3. Find the ownership mechanism. Read server/auth.ts and server/lib/ownership.ts and server/routes.ts to learn what is applied globally versus per route.
4. For each data-returning route, decide whether ownership is enforced, and by what. Note routes that use their own Router() instance, because global middleware may not reach them.
5. Report every route that returns or mutates scoped data without an enforced ownership check.
