## Part 2: extract brands

table, `insertBrandSchema`, `InsertBrand`, `Brand`. Confirm the boundary
yourself before cutting. Move them to `shared/schema/brands.ts`.

the first cross-module reference in this split and it must stay one directional:
`identity` knows nothing about `brands`.

value to them, the same way it currently imports `users` from `identity`.

Follow the barrel pattern already in `shared/schema.ts`: `export *`, not a named
re-export. 25 files use `import * as schema`.
